Installing

  1. Unzip. Put the .component in ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components and the .vst3 in ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3 (in Finder press ⌘⇧G and paste the path).
  2. It isn't Apple-notarized yet, so macOS will quarantine it. Open Terminal and paste:
xattr -cr ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/*.component ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/*.vst3
  1. Fully quit and reopen your DAW. It's under Satin Press.
