Beta Notes

Installing the beta

These builds are fresh off the workbench and not yet Apple-notarized, so your Mac will quarantine them on first download. Three minutes fixes it:

1 · Unzip, then copy the .component file into ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components and the .vst3 into ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3 (in Finder: Cmd+Shift+G, paste the path).

2 · Open Terminal and paste:
xattr -cr ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/*.component ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/*.vst3

3 · Fully quit and reopen your DAW — both live under SP. The INSTALL.txt inside each download has the same steps.
