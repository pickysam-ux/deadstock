Installing

These builds aren't Apple-notarized yet, so macOS will quarantine them on first download. Three minutes fixes it:

  1. Unzip. Copy the .component into ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components and the .vst3 into ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3. In Finder press ⌘⇧G and paste the path.
  2. Open Terminal, paste this, press return:
xattr -cr ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/*.component ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/*.vst3
  1. Fully quit and reopen your DAW. All three live under Satin Press.

Each download also contains an INSTALL.txt with the same steps.
